Which hat do you reach for first? Think of one situation this week where your team needs you to use a different role. What will you say or do differently?

Several situations combine roles. The best fit comes from the main purpose of the action, rather than a single word such as “ask”, “plan” or “explain”. These are roles you move between, rather than three types of people. A real conversation may include all three; this challenge asks which role is most evident in the action described.

Manager

Leader

Coach

Develop someone’s thinking, capability and ownership.

The same action can serve different purposes. Look at what the manager is trying to achieve in this situation.

Facilitator notes

Invite participants to explain their reasoning before revealing a choice in a group discussion. Treat the answer as the best fit for the stated action. Explore credible alternatives rather than teaching that a behaviour belongs exclusively to one role. Use the final reflection to connect the distinctions to participants’ own teams.
